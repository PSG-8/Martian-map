# Decisions
- **Vanilla JS, no framework/build** — runs anywhere, instant deploys; keep it unless the app grows a lot.
- **Supabase RPC-only access with RLS** — the browser never touches tables directly.
- **3D only on capable desktops** (v25) — phones lagged and banners didn't show; phones get pre-rendered images with a CSS zoom.
- **Videos hosted in the site repo (`media/`)**, served by Vercel; service worker bypasses them so iOS range requests work.
- **Explainer narration: male voice** (user's request), Kokoro open-source TTS because HeyGen speech credit ran out.
- **Character art: Figma AI image generation (gpt-image)**, motion via HeyGen image-to-video (free plan: 3 clips). Cut-out parallax was removed because it looked like doubled characters.
- **Team highlight is subtle** — only a star (user found the blue card "too much").
- **Questions page is framed as asking nurses/experts**, not as answers.
- **Results follow lab practice (v28)** — a value just outside the range is "slightly high/low" and stays *normal with tips*; only clearly abnormal values ask for a doctor; *urgent* only above true critical limits. Women and children have their own ranges.
- **Money split (v28, planning figures)** — visit fee 5.000: nurse 3.000 (+ level bonus), courier 1.000, kit 0.500, platform 0.500; labs get 65% of test prices; consult 4.000 (doctor 75%); home dental 12.000 (dentist 65%). The previous setting paid the whole visit fee to the nurse, leaving the company nothing.
- **Video consults use meet.jit.si links** — free, no accounts; chat consults use WhatsApp.
- **Videos & pictures (v29)** — Pixar/Disney style, realistic lighting; still pictures with camera moves (no AI video, so no animation glitches); English narration (Kokoro) + Arabic subtitles + light music.
- **Modesty rules for all art** — full Gulf-style hijab for women; no kumma with medical uniforms; no people printed on merchandise.
- **Logo (v29)** — the approved hexagon + flask is the only logo (the v28 roof/drop logo was dropped). Animated as a hologram on the site.
- **GSAP loaded after the first paint** (v31) — rich motion without slowing phones; if GSAP is slow or reduced motion is on, the page works without it.
- **Kits only when every item is self-collected** (v31) — one blood test in the cart means a nurse visit; keeps the flow simple and safe.
- **Discounts only on the server** (v31) — stamps, coupons and referral rewards are computed in `order_create`; the phone only shows a preview.
- **Companies never see medical values** (v31) — camp results are fit / needs follow-up / retest only.
- **Official residency medical stays with MOH** (v31) — iLab@home offers pre-checks and yearly checks, and says so on every related screen.
- **Explainer narration now female** (v31, user's request; men's voice felt harsh): Kokoro `af_heart`.
- **Never hide content ahead of time** (v32) — animations start only when an element reaches the screen; anything that never gets there is simply visible. ScrollTrigger pre-hiding and `content-visibility` left whole sections blank on some computers.
- **Decorative animation must rest** (v32) — the hologram logo plays a few seconds and stops; endless SVG/box-shadow animations cost the main thread on weak phones.
- **The newest tap wins** (v32) — leaving a screen cancels its pending reads instead of waiting up to ~20 s.
- **Noor is SVG, not 3D** (v32) — the owner asked for a light 3D-feel character that never slows the site; SVG + CSS transforms gives that at a few KB.
- **Arabic layout puts the banner scene on the left** (v32) — the text sits on the reading side and never covers the picture.
- **Three videos, one job each** (v32) — idea, how to use the app (real screens), and a cinematic day-in-the-life film; no real actors or AI video (no paid credits), all drawn with the site's own characters.
- **Nurse gender rule** (v33, owner): male nurse → men; female nurse → women and children under 12. Enforced in the booking screen and in the database (trigger, order_create, auto-assign).
- **The customer never pays for our mistakes** (v33): damaged/lost samples → free re-collection or full refund + apology gift; iLab@home is the single responsible party and settles with partners afterwards. What we do not cover is written plainly on `#/guarantee`.
- **Spoken names use phonemes** (v33): English TTS mispronounces Arabic names (Noor→"Nor", Maryam→"Merry-am"), so names are fed as IPA and checked by transcription.
- **Demo accounts see demo data only** (v33 security): the demo password is public, so a demo admin/doctor/nurse/courier/lab must never reach a real person's data. Enforced in the database (`_demo_ok`), not in the screens.
- **Partners are approved by a person** (v33): automatic approval after 2 minutes was a risk (a stranger could sign up as a nurse and receive home addresses). Testers use the demo partner accounts.
- **Self-service profile fields are a short allow-list** (v33): everything else in `meta` (fleet manager, stamps, referral, manual mode, gender for partners) is set by the system or the owner.
- **Hadith and Qur'an are spoken by a calm male Arabic voice** (v33, owner): English narration introduces it, the Arabic is recited, the English meaning is written. HeyGen's free voice allowance was used up, so the voice is generated locally (open-source model) and checked by Arabic transcription.
