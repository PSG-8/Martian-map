## Phase 1 — followers first (before posting)
1. Share `2_story_follow_qr.png` in WhatsApp groups/status (family, class, college) with:
```
مشروعنا الطلابي آي لاب@هوم 🩺🏠
فحوصات مخبرية تجيك لين البيت — قريباً في شمال الشرقية.
تابعونا وادعمونا 💙 instagram.com/ilabathome
```
2. Every team member follows, turns on notifications, and shares the account in their story.
3. Print the QR story as an A4 poster for college / the presentation day.
4. Follow local accounts in the area (Ibra, Al Mudhaybi, Sinaw…), health pages and student clubs; reply to comments.
5. Aim for ~100 followers before the first post (so it gets likes in the first hour).
