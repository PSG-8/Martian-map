# Design system
- Brand: iLab@home — wordmark with "i" and "@" in blue, "home" lowercase. Logo (v28): blue rounded square, white roof over a white blood drop with a blue cross, orange dot top-right (`ART.markSvg()` in `js/art.js`; options bg/fg/cross/dot for one-colour versions). Brand page with all versions and merch mockups: `#/brand`.
- Colours: royal blue #2563EB (lead), navy #0D1628 (text), teal #14B8A6 (support), orange #F97316 (accent), red #EF4444 (urgent only), light ground #F5F8FD.
- Type: Poppins (headings), Inter (body), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic).
- Phones first: big tap targets, no heavy effects on mobile, test at 390x844.
- Presentations: fixed 1600x900 stage, one idea per slide, text ≥ 20px on stage.
