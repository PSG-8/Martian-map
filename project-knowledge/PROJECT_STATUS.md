# Project status — last updated 2026-11-26

## v42 (built 2026-11-26) — every phone, every account (owner's request after a Samsung user reported layout errors and dead buttons)
A checker now lives in the repo: `tools/qa/` (see its README; `tools/qa/run-all.sh` quick, `run-all.sh full` = every screen x every account x 20 devices). Found and fixed:
- **Back button**: redirects (signed-in user opening sign-in, a page an account cannot open, after sign-in, session ended) added a history entry, so the phone's Back landed on the redirect again and bounced forward: Back looked dead. Redirects now replace the entry; the app's Back uses the real history when there is one and the logical parent otherwise (never leaves the site from a WhatsApp/Instagram link). Error and empty screens have a Back too. The previous version failed 11 back-button checks; this one passes all 23.
- **Blank site on some phones**: browsers that block storage (Instagram/WhatsApp in-app browsers, strict private modes) threw on start and showed a white page; a damaged/old saved cart did the same. `js/store.js` (loaded first) gives an in-memory stand-in; the cart and cached catalog are validated.
- **Owner on laptops**: the side menu (17 items for the owner) was taller than a 1366x768 / 1280x720 screen and could not scroll: "More" and the account card were unreachable. It now scrolls and is compact on short screens.
- **320 px phones** (Galaxy S8/S9+ with display zoom, iPhone SE, large text): top bar, "Add to cart", Instagram follow, "Install the app", company share button and result rows stuck out sideways; buttons and chips now wrap, top bar shrinks (wordmark hidden only below 310 px). Owner overview text was squeezed into one-word lines next to its button. Fold covers/landscape: last control no longer under the bottom bar (space = real bar height). Noor steps aside at the page bottom so she never covers footer links. Footer/"see all" links get bigger tap areas on touch screens.
- **Squeezed text** (new `tools/qa/squeeze.js`): 187 spots where a sentence was forced into a one-word-per-line column next to a button or picture, many on the 384 px Galaxy S24 itself (notification card, patient home self-kit/companies cards at 45 px, test cards, consultation cards, owner card, team/roles/goals/guarantee cards, tablet user table). Cards with sentences now go one column on phones, test cards put price + "Add to cart" on their own row, text wraps above buttons. 0 left at 320/360/384/412/768/1194/1366 px.
- **Forced dark mode** (Samsung Internet, MIUI, Huawei): `color-scheme: light only`; Samsung may still darken pages (it ignores the opt-out in some versions), checked readable.
- Older browsers: support-topic chips mark the choice without CSS `:has()`; no `Array.at` in the 3D banner.
